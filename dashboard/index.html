How the radar scores

Implied value = TVL × 0.22 × growth × freshness × breadth. Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −80%…+200% and weighted 0.6. Freshness is the new-pair signal: a listing under 30 days old scores 1.60, under 90 days 1.45, under 180 days 1.30, under a year 1.15, under two years 1.00, older 0.90; an unknown listing date scores 1.00. Breadth adds 0.02 per extra chain, up to four. No protocol name is ever special-cased: the same function scores every row.

“Records no token” means DefiLlama's record carries no market cap, CoinGecko id or symbol for the protocol — never that it has none. A protocol on this board may still have a token its record does not carry. The board is the entry's own claim, not a prediction that a token will be issued, or when.
